How to convert ECTS to US credits

A common rough estimate is 2 ECTS = 1 US semester credit. Since one US semester credit is generally equivalent to 1.5 quarter credits, the corresponding rough estimate is 2 ECTS = 1.5 US quarter credits. Actual recognition is determined by the receiving institution.

Frequently asked questions

Is 60 ECTS equal to 30 US credits?

Under the common 2:1 planning estimate, yes: 60 ECTS is approximately 30 US semester credits. Your university may calculate transfer credits differently.

Can I use this for university admission?

Use it to plan and compare credit loads, but check your target university’s official transfer-credit or international admissions policy before applying.
